##### de.wikipedia – Pierce Brosnan
Pierce Brendan Brosnan, OBE (* 16. Mai 1953 in Drogheda, Irland) ist ein irischer Schauspieler, Filmproduzent, Umweltschutz-Aktivist und Maler. Seit 2004 besitzt er auch die US-amerikanische Staatsbürgerschaft. Er wurde in den 1980er-Jahren mit der Fernsehserie Remington Steele und ab 1995 als fünfter Darsteller von James Bond bekannt, den er bis 2002 in vier Filmen verkörperte.


== Leben und Karriere ==

Pierce Brosnan wurde 1953 als Sohn des Tischlers Thomas Brosnan und dessen Frau May Smith im irischen Drogheda geboren. Er lebte anschließend zwölf Jahre lang in Navan. Nach der Trennung seiner Eltern zog Brosnans Mutter nach London, um dort als Krankenschwester zu arbeiten. Ab diesem Zeitpunkt, im Alter von vier Jahren, lebte er bei seinen Großeltern Philip und Kathleen Smith. Nach deren Tod lebte er zunächst bei Tante und Onkel, später auch bei anderen Verwandten. 1964 verließ er Irland und zog zu seiner Mutter und ihrem neuen Mann William Carmichael, einem britischen Veteranen des Zweiten Weltkriegs, in das schottische Dorf Longniddry. Brosnan akzeptierte den neuen Mann seiner Mutter schnell als Vaterfigur. Später besuchte er die Elliott School, eine Mittelschule in Putney (London).
Nachdem er mit 16 die Schule verlassen hatte, arbeitete er als Kunstmaler und begann eine Ausbildung am Central Saint Martins College of Art and Design. 1969 sah er bei einem Workshop im Oval House Theater einen Feuerschlucker, woraufhin er beschloss, dies zu erlernen. Ein Zirkusmitarbeiter sah ihn als Straßenkünstler und engagierte ihn für drei Jahre. Ab 1973 studierte er Schauspiel am Drama Centre London. Um seine Ausbildung zu finanzieren, arbeitete er nebenbei als Assistent des Bühnenmanagers am Royal Theatre.
1977 wurde der Dramatiker Tennessee Williams auf Brosnan aufmerksam und besetzte ihn für die britische Erstaufführung seines Stücks Red Devil Battery Sign im Roundhouse Theatre in London. Es folgten Auftritte in Filumina und Warte, bis es dunkel ist sowie kleinere Statistenjobs beim Fernsehen, unter anderem in Guy Hamiltons Agatha-Christie-Verfilmung Mord im Spiegel, wo er in einer Komparsenrolle kurz an der Seite von Elizabeth Taylor auftritt. 1981 spielte er die Hauptrolle in der TV-Miniserie Die Manions in Amerika.
1980 spielte Cassandra Harris, seine spätere Ehefrau, eine Nebenrolle in dem James-Bond-Film James Bond 007 – In tödlicher Mission. Anlässlich eines gemeinsamen Essens des Filmteams stellte sie Brosnan dem Produzenten Albert R. Broccoli als ihren Verlobten vor.
1982 gelang ihm mit der Krimiserie Remington Steele der internationale Durchbruch. 1986 wurde die Produktion nach vier Staffeln (88 Folgen) eingestellt, da es Probleme mit den Produzenten und auch mit Brosnans Partnerin Stephanie Zimbalist gegeben hatte. Zur selben Zeit befand sich der Produzent Albert R. Broccoli auf der Suche nach einem Nachfolger für Roger Moore als James-Bond-Darsteller. Brosnan, dem die Rolle als einem der ersten angeboten wurde, musste jedoch ablehnen, da er bei Drehbeginn von Der Hauch des Todes noch bei Remington Steele unter Vertrag stand und die Produzenten ihn nun doch für eine weitere fünfte Staffel haben wollten. Dass von dieser fünften Staffel schließlich nur sechs Folgen gedreht wurden und er somit James Bond schon damals hätte spielen können, führte zum endgültigen Zerwürfnis mit den Remington-Steele-Produzenten. An seiner Stelle wurde die Rolle des James Bond mit Timothy Dalton besetzt.

In der Zwischenzeit war er mit Filmen wie Das vierte Protokoll, Die Täuscher und Mister Johnson bekannt geworden. 1992 und 1993 hatte er mit Der Rasenmähermann und der Komödie Mrs. Doubtfire auch kommerzielle Erfolge. 1994 erhielt Brosnan doch noch die Rolle des Geheimagenten James Bond. GoldenEye spielte weltweit über 350 Millionen Dollar ein und war der bis dahin erfolgreichste Film dieser Reihe. Es folgten Der Morgen stirbt nie (1997), Die Welt ist nicht genug (1999) und Stirb an einem anderen Tag (2002). Daneben war Brosnan unter anderem auch in Tim Burtons Mars Attacks! und Dante’s Peak zu sehen. Seit 1998 betätigt er sich mit seiner Firma Irish Dreamtime auch im Bereich der Filmproduktion. Einen Erfolg konnte er dabei zum Beispiel mit dem Remake Die Thomas Crown Affäre landen.
2004 kündigte Brosnan an, er werde James Bond in Zukunft nicht mehr verkörpern. Ursprünglich war ein fünfter Film mit Brosnan in der Titelrolle geplant. Im selben Jahr erhielt er die US-amerikanische Staatsbürgerschaft. 2008 war er zusammen mit Meryl Streep in der Verfilmung des ABBA-Musicals Mamma Mia! zu sehen, wofür ihm die Goldene Himbeere als schlechtester Nebendarsteller verliehen wurde. Es folgten weitere Film- und Fernsehrollen in ganz verschiedenen Genres, von Komödien, über Actionfilme bis hin zu Dramen. Seine Rolle in der Romanverfilmung Der Ghostwriter brachte ihm die Anerkennung der Kritiker ein. Sein Schaffen umfasst mehr als 90 Produktionen.
Im Mai 2023 eröffnete Brosnan seine erste Einzelausstellung mit dem Titel So Many Dreams (So viele Träume), eine Sammlung von Gemälden und Zeichnungen, die der Schauspieler von den 1980er Jahren bis heute geschaffen hat.

„(Das Kunstwerk) ist biografisch. Ich habe versucht, die Memoiren zu schreiben, aber es ist so langweilig. Man muss sich nur im Kopf und im Herzen und in der Erinnerung an das Leben vertiefen. Und so haben diese Bilder definitiv eine Geschichte davon, wer ich war, wo ich war und wann ich war“, sagte der Schauspieler.


== Synchronisation ==
In der deutschen Synchronisation ist Frank Glaubrecht seit 1985 Brosnans Standardstimme. Ausnahmen: Im Fernsehmehrteiler In 80 Tagen um die Welt (1989) wurde er von Lutz Mackensy, in Mrs. Doubtfire – Das stachelige Kindermädchen (1993) von Reinhard Kuhnert, in Fast Charlie (2023) von Jürgen Heinrich und in The Unholy Trinity (2024) sowie in Black Bag – Doppeltes Spiel (2025) von Marcus Off synchronisiert.


== Familie ==
Brosnan heiratete 1980 die Schauspielerin Cassandra Harris und hat mit ihr einen Sohn (* 1983). 1987 erkrankte Harris an Eierstockkrebs. Nach ihrem Tod 1991 adoptierte Brosnan Tochter und Sohn aus ihrer ersten Ehe und gründete eine Stiftung für die Krebsforschung. Die Tochter starb 2013 mit 41 Jahren ebenfalls an den Folgen von Eierstockkrebs.
2001 heiratete Brosnan die Journalistin Keely Shaye Smith, mit der er zwei Söhne hat. Brosnan lebt in Malibu und auf der hawaiischen Insel Kauai.


== Engagement für Tier- und Umweltschutz ==
Der Vegetarier Pierce Brosnan ist Teil des Beratergremiums der Meeresschutzorganisation Sea Shepherd, und ihren Gründer Paul Watson bezeichnet er als Freund. Als Watson 2012 verhaftet wurde, setzte sich Brosnan für dessen Freilassung ein. In seinem Bestreben, Wale und Meere zu schützen, arbeitete Brosnan neben seiner Tätigkeit bei Sea Shepherd auch mit der Meeresschutzvereinigung Oceana sowie dem International Fund for Animal Welfare, der Humane Society of the United States und der Whaleman Foundation zusammen.
1995 boykottierte Brosnan die Frankreichpremiere von James Bond 007: GoldenEye, um Solidarität mit Greenpeace und deren Protest gegen das französische Atomwaffentestprogramm im Südpazifik zu zeigen. 2004 wurde er von der Sustainable Style Foundation zum Best Dressed Environmentalist gewählt.
Gemeinsam mit seiner Frau und einigen internationalen Stars wie Daryl Hannah, Cindy Crawford und Halle Berry setzte sich Brosnan erfolgreich gegen den Bau einer Erdgasgewinnungsanlage an der Küste Malibus ein. 2007 legte der damalige Gouverneur Kaliforniens, Arnold Schwarzenegger, sein Veto gegen den Bau der Anlage ein.
2007 wurde Brosnan die Goldene Kamera für sein Umweltengagement verliehen. 2019 lobte er die schwedische Klimaschutzaktivistin Greta Thunberg und rief dazu auf, sich an ihr ein Beispiel zu nehmen. Gleichzeitig kritisierte er Donald Trump dafür, den Klimawandel zu leugnen.

##### en.wikipedia – Crookhaven (TV series)
Crookhaven is a British television drama series based on J. J. Arcanjo's book series of the same name. Set in a secret school for aspiring crooks, the show follows the adventures of young pickpocket Gabriel Avery. The series premiered on 22 March 2026 on BBC One, CBBC and BBC iPlayer. In July 2026, the BBC commissioned a second series.


== Synopsis ==
Gabriel Avery, a young pickpocket, is recruited to study at the Crookhaven School, which aims to train their students in crooked skills but guide them to use their skills for good. He finds himself among eight new recruits at the school, one of whom is his arch-rival Penelope, the daughter of the headmaster, Caspian Lockett. The new recruits compete to be the best of the year and win the coveted Crooked Cup.  However, nothing is as it appears in the school, and along the way Gabriel discovers something surprising about himself and has to confront a dangerous enemy called The Nameless, who poses a threat to the school.

== History ==
Ade and his twin brother, Ede, are highly skilled “white-hat” hackers. Their father was badly injured by faulty equipment, which motivated the twins to expose corruption and wrongdoing. They became famous online for exposing companies such as Con-Oil and Hydro-Cell, although they keep their identities secret.

== The School For Thieves ==

=== Ade, Ede and Sneaks ===
When Gabriel first meets Ade Okoro, Ade is using Sneaks, a robot that he and Ede built. Sneaks can:

* Dive underwater
* Travel across land
* Fly
* Perform technological tasks

The twins treat Sneaks almost like a pet, although they cannot agree whether Sneaks is a boy or a girl.

Ade and Ede are famous online for their hacking work, including exposing Con-Oil and Hydro-Cell. However, they try to remain anonymous so that people cannot identify them or trace their activities.

Ade and Ede constantly argue over small things, including who should introduce them. Ade is older by only three minutes, which he uses as an excuse to claim seniority.

Despite their constant bickering, they work extremely well together. Their coordination becomes especially impressive when they are working as the famous hacking duo known as the Brothers Crim.

Ade is affectionate and protective toward Ede, even though he frequently teases and mocks him.

=== Meeting Gabriel and Penelope ===
Penelope immediately recognises the twins because of their famous hacking activities. She also knows about their father and how his injury helped motivate them to expose wrongdoing.

When Penelope talks about Whisper, Ade asks Ede to give him 50p because he had previously told Ede that “Whisper’s code was too beautiful to be a man’s.”

Whisper also secretly gives Penelope a special USB drive disguised as an earring. Ade becomes fascinated by the technology.

Ade and Ede are excited about starting at Crookhaven. They are especially interested in meeting Whisper because they suspect she may have been responsible for recruiting them.

The twins joke about wearing suits to impress her. Their ideas quickly become ridiculous when Ede suggests hacking a delivery drone and redirecting it to Crookhaven.

During the class sorting, they deliberately interfere with their results by shaking hands aggressively. Their palms therefore show a confusing mixture of red and blue.

Whisper realises what they have done but is too tired to argue with them, so she places both twins in Class 1B.

The twins are delighted to discover that they will be classmates with Gabriel and Penelope. They run over, hug them and excitedly declare that the year will be great.

=== Ade's First Day ===
Ade has already checked the timetable and tells Gabriel that their first lesson is Deception, which Gabriel immediately likes the sound of.

He is also excited about the announcement of the Break-in. Like the other students, he is interested in winning the 50 points, although the students must wait until the second term before attempting it.

Ade already knows more about Crookhaven's history than Gabriel.

When Villette discusses the Nameless, Ade immediately notices that something is wrong. He identifies her as Villette Harkness and says he is not convinced that she ever had the plot in the first place.

At lunch, Ade explains that although he and Ede are highly skilled white-hat hackers, they know very little about the Nameless.

They deliberately avoid investigating them too closely because the Nameless are extremely dangerous.

Unlike Crookhaven students, who use their skills for schemes and challenges, the Nameless:

* Kidnap people
* Hurt people
* Kill people for money
* Do not use their abilities to put things right

The twins explain that the Nameless:

* Have no crew name
* Never use codenames
* Never repeat the same con
* Are extremely difficult to track

Ade describes them as ghosts.

=== Ade's Humour and Personality ===
Ade is constantly joking and teasing the people around him.

During lunch, he complains that his legs are exhausted after Miss Jericho decides not to use desks. Penelope becomes uncomfortable with the joke.

He also teases Gabriel and Penelope for eating quickly. Ade strongly disagrees with Penelope's belief that food is simply fuel because he thinks food is one of the best things in life.

He even jokes that Penelope might be a robot because of how emotionlessly she eats.

Ade is also playful in the mornings, complaining that Penelope is far too energetic and that nobody should be so cheerful so early.

During their first Tricks of the Trade class, Ade notices that Gabriel and Mr Velasquez look somewhat alike. He jokingly asks Gabriel what talent he was recruited for.

When Gabriel proudly says pickpocketing, Ade finds the answer amusing.

The twins struggle during the walk through the woods. Ade jokes that he is having a heart attack, while Ede jokes about what would happen if Ade died and they had to change their tag.

When choosing their garden patches, they continue fooling around. Ede even throws a caterpillar at Ade, causing him to run away.

Penelope observes that although the twins could eventually become an extremely skilled hacking team, they are much less coordinated in person.

=== Forgery and Other Lessons ===
Ade and Ede misbehave during Forgery by hiding paintbrushes in their sleeves and secretly flicking each other with them whenever Palombo is not looking.

Ade later asks Penelope why Caspian keeps talking to Villette. He is curious about their repeated conversations, although Penelope does not give him an answer.

Ade is extremely excited when he learns that Whisper will teach their next class.

He tells Caspian that he and Ede have been waiting their whole lives for the opportunity to be taught by her.

Whisper quickly demonstrates why the twins admire her. Ade and Ede work through the technological equipment at astonishing speed and do not need to copy her.

They work together in complete silence, moving their hands in perfect coordination without touching each other.

Gabriel realises that there is a huge difference between the joking twins he knows and the highly skilled hackers known as the Brothers Crim.

=== The Twins' Earrings ===
Ade is one of the few Merits allowed to keep his skull-and-crossbones earrings.

He jokes that they are “hunting for treasure” when they sit at their keyboards, which leads to another argument with Ede about online piracy.

Ade and Ede decide to turn their mother's homemade jollof rice into a business after their first supply becomes extremely popular among the Crooklings.

They plan to sell portions to students and charge Legacies more than other students.

Demand becomes so high that they need to contact their mother at least twice a week for more supplies, despite Crookhaven's restrictions on calling home.

Because the island's signal scrambling blocks most modern communication devices, the twins find a way to amplify an old radio signal.

They even develop a secret method of getting the radio onto the island using Sneaks.

=== Crimnastics and Black Thunder ===
Ade and Ede take part in the class's Crimnastics disaster.

They insist on completing the assault course together but collide on the monkey bars and end up fighting before finishing with a terrible time.

Later, Ede invents the nickname “Black Thunder.” Ade mocks him mercilessly, but Gabriel notices that Ede secretly enjoys the nickname.

Ade and Ede tell Gabriel about the rumour that Penelope's mother was taken by the Nameless.

They speculate that the USB drives Whisper gives Penelope might contain information about her disappearance.

Ade later explains that Whisper has asked to tutor the twins because of their exceptional hacking abilities, even though they struggle more in other subjects.

The twins also reveal that their listening devices have picked up conversations around Crookhaven. This eventually gives them information about Penelope's mother and the Nameless.

=== Sneaks and Communication ===
Ade and Ede spend time trying to tune Sneaks to the correct frequency so they can contact their mother.

Crookhaven's poor signal and bad weather make this difficult.

Ade also helps Gabriel investigate whether Sneaks could be used against Orwell, the crow-camera.

He immediately understands Gabriel's idea and confirms that Sneaks has a two-metre electromagnetic pulse (EMP).

Ade becomes angry when he discovers that Decome destroyed Gabriel's stored food.

He and Ede decide that Decome's crew will not be allowed to buy their jollof rice for a week.

However, they quickly realise that this could hurt their business because Decome's group is one of their biggest customers.

=== The Mysterious Recording ===
While trying to contact their mother through Sneaks, the twins accidentally capture a mysterious conversation.

Ade keeps the recording because their mother often makes promises and later backs out of them.

The twins enhance the recording, making the male voice clearer.

Ade thinks the speaker sounds like Mr Velasquez because of his Spanish accent, although neither twin is certain because the voices are not identical enough.

Ade immediately agrees to join Gabriel and Amira's Break-in team.

He recognises that their abilities complement one another:

* Gabriel: Excellent pickpocket
* Amira: Exceptionally fast
* Ade and Ede: Highly skilled with technology

Ade initially refuses to support recruiting Penelope. He argues that one Legacy is enough and worries that Penelope would try to take control of the whole operation.

He eventually agrees that Gabriel can try to convince her.

When Penelope refuses, Ade encourages Gabriel to recruit Ji-a instead, believing she would be a good fit.

=== Preparing for the Break-in ===
Ade and Ede continue developing Sneaks and handling the technological side of the team.

Their usual bickering continues, including an argument over whether the expression is “chalk and cheese” or “chalk and chips.”

Despite this, the twins remain confident that Sneaks is ready for the Break-in.

They are also impressed when Gabriel reveals his memory ability and accept him as the team's leader without much resistance.

During the Break-in, Ade and Ede handle the technology.

They use Sneaks' EMP to disable Orwell, the crow-camera.

The temporary shutdown gives Amira enough time to complete the theft.

The twins also record the entire operation and project the footage for the Crooklings to see.

Whisper recognises how clever their timing was because the EMP lasts just long enough without making her suspicious.

For their successful work, the twins earn 50 points each and the right to ask Caspian a truthful question.

=== Sacrificing Sneaks ===
When Adria triggers the library's emergency system, Ade and Ede immediately help Gabriel escape.

They use Sneaks to hold the door open while Gabriel gets through the narrowing gap.

However, Sneaks becomes trapped in the crushing door and is badly damaged.

The twins cry out in pain when their robot is damaged, but they have put themselves and their equipment at risk to save Gabriel.

Ade and Ede insist on holding a funeral for Sneaks because they believe the robot sacrificed itself to save Gabriel.

Their dramatic grief leads to another argument with Penelope, who is mainly concerned with getting to lunch.

The twins later promise to rebuild Sneaks.

=== Helping Gabriel Investigate the Nameless ===
Ade and Ede reveal that they already knew about Penelope's mother because their listening devices had picked up conversations around Crookhaven.

They promise to help investigate the Nameless, although they worry that the Nameless may now be angry with them because they accidentally recorded one of their conversations.

Ade and Ede leave for the holidays still arguing and joking about rebuilding Sneaks.

Despite all their arguments, teasing and mischief, their loyalty to one another and their friends remains clear.

They also promise to return the following term with even more jollof rice.

== The School For Thieves ==

=== Caspian’s Role and Introduction to Gabriel ===
Caspian first tests Gabriel at the train station by pretending to be a distracted, easy target. When Gabriel steals his wallet, Caspian secretly takes Gabriel’s unusual 2p coin, using it to lead Gabriel to Crookhaven.

At Crookhaven, Caspian reveals that he is one of the school’s leaders and comes from a long family line of crooks. He explains that the school trains talented people to use their unusual abilities to correct injustices that ordinary systems cannot address. He offers Gabriel a place at the school while assuring him that he can still communicate with and visit his grandmother.

=== Welcoming the New Students ===
Caspian helps organise the arrival of the first-year Legacies and operates the mechanism that raises the hidden path through the lake. He welcomes Whisper back from her travels, though she warns him that what she discovered is worse than expected.

He also deliberately keeps Penelope with the Merits instead of allowing her to join the Legacy welcome. Whisper notices this and jokes that Caspian has already embarrassed her.

During the main welcome speech, Caspian explains that Crookhaven accepts people whose “crooked” talents are rejected by ordinary society. The students were chosen not simply for being skilled thieves, hackers or lockpickers, but because they used those skills for good reasons—such as surviving, protecting others, exposing corruption or recovering stolen property.

His central message is that Crookhaven teaches students how to do wrong in order to eventually put the world right. He demonstrates the school's connection to generations of crooks by throwing his pebble into the lake, causing thousands of alumni pebbles to light up beneath the water.

== The Break-in Challenge ==
Caspian introduces the first-years to the Break-in, a challenge designed to test their ability to apply Crookhaven skills in a real situation. Students must enter a protected room, steal an object and escape undetected.

He emphasises that anyone can steal, but a truly skilled crook can steal something without the victim even realising it is gone. The students are shocked when Caspian reveals that the target is his own office.

=== Discovering Gabriel’s Abilities ===
Caspian repeatedly tests Gabriel and eventually discovers that he has a photographic memory. Gabriel successfully remembers objects that the Gardeners have moved, despite avoiding the school's memory game.

Caspian recognises that Gabriel's memory is both a gift and a burden. He is especially impressed when Gabriel notices a repaired fountain that even Caspian had overlooked, describing Gabriel's observation as genuinely devious.

Caspian also explains that Penelope genuinely likes Gabriel, Ade and Ede. He is happy that she has finally found friends her own age because, having grown up at Crookhaven among older students, she has had to mature too quickly.

=== Leon and Marianna’s Disappearance ===
When Leon and Marianna disappear, Caspian admits that he does not know what has happened to them. However, he reassures Gabriel that the entire Crookhaven alumni network is searching for them and believes that even highly skilled Tier One crooks cannot completely disappear from the Underworld's attention.

Gabriel remains suspicious despite Caspian's reassurance.

== The Break-in’s Unexpected Success ==
Caspian initially laughs when Gabriel claims that the crew stole something from his office because he believes nothing is missing. His confidence vanishes when he discovers that a painting he thought was original has actually been replaced with Penelope's forgery.

He is deeply impressed by her work, calling it “absolutely … exquisite,” but becomes genuinely angry when he realises the full extent of the crew's Break-in.

Despite this, he awards the crew 50 points each and grants them their privilege of asking a truthful question, while warning them to use the opportunity wisely.

=== Gabriel’s Parents and the Nameless ===
Caspian later reveals major secrets about Gabriel's background. Gabriel was recruited because of his exceptional pickpocketing ability, but also because he was connected to Luciano Lopes and the Nameless.

The charred coin Gabriel possesses is revealed to be a Mark belonging to the Nameless. Whisper helped investigate Gabriel's background after the alumni network discovered the coin.

Caspian also admits that he deliberately made Gabriel famous in the Underworld so that his biological parents would eventually hear about him.

=== Penelope’s Mother ===
Caspian reveals that Penelope's mother was kidnapped by the Nameless three years earlier. They did not demand money or anything else; they kidnapped her simply to prove that they could defeat Caspian.

This explains why Caspian made an exception for Gabriel: in his twenty years at Crookhaven, he had never personally recruited another student in this way.

=== Adria’s Infiltration and Increased Security ===
When Caspian discovers that Adria Vivas, a member of the Nameless and Gabriel's biological mother, has infiltrated Crookhaven, he immediately increases the school's security.

He sends Yuri Malenko, one of Crookhaven's best trackers, to search for Gabriel's parents and doubles the number of Gardeners patrolling the grounds at night during the final two weeks of term.

Caspian questions Gabriel about the infiltration, but Gabriel cannot provide much information because he does not know how Adria entered the school or where she and Luciano have gone.

== History ==
Ede’s father was badly injured because of faulty equipment. This motivated Ede and his twin brother, Ade, to become hackers so they could expose companies involved in corruption and wrongdoing.

When Gabriel first meets Ede Okoro, he is using a robot called Sneaks, which he and Ade built themselves. Sneaks can dive underwater, move across land and fly.

Ede and Ade explain that they are “white hats”: hackers who find weaknesses in computer systems so they can be fixed. They also use their skills to expose corruption and wrongdoing.

The brothers are famous online but try to remain anonymous. Being recognised could reveal their identities and leave traces of their activities. They treat Sneaks almost like a pet, although they cannot even agree about whether Sneaks is a boy or a girl.

== The School For Thieves ==

=== Ede and Ade’s Relationship ===
Ede and Ade constantly argue about small things, including who should introduce them because Ade is older by only three minutes. Despite their arguments, they work extremely well together.

Penelope immediately recognises them because of their famous hacking jobs, including exposing Con-Oil and Hydro-Cell. She also knows that their father helped motivate them to become hackers.

When Penelope talks about Whisper, Ade asks Ede to give him 50p because he had previously told Ede that Whisper’s code was too beautiful to be a man’s.

The twins are excited about meeting Whisper because they believe she may have recruited them to Crookhaven. They joke about wearing suits to impress her, although Ede soon suggests something much more ridiculous: hacking into a delivery drone and redirecting it to Crookhaven.

=== Arriving at Crookhaven ===
During the class sorting, the twins deliberately interfere with their results by shaking hands aggressively. Their palms show a confusing mixture of red and blue.

Whisper clearly realises what they have done but is too tired to argue, so she places both of them in Class 1B.

The twins are delighted to discover that they will be in the same class as Gabriel and Penelope. They run over, hug them and excitedly declare that the year is going to be great.

Ede is particularly excited about his first day. He has already checked the timetable and tells Gabriel that their first lesson is Deception, which Gabriel immediately likes the sound of.

The twins also join the other students for the announcement of the Break-in. Like everyone else, Ede is excited by the challenge and the possibility of winning fifty points, although they must wait until the second term before attempting it.

Ede already knows more about some of Crookhaven’s history than Gabriel. During History of Crooks, he quietly tells Gabriel that Lulu Cheng is rumoured to be descended from Ching Shih, the Pirate Queen.

When Villette begins talking about the Nameless, Ede immediately realises that something is wrong and says that she has “lost the plot.”

At lunch, the Brothers Crim join Gabriel and complain about Miss Jericho’s decision not to use desks. Ede suggests teasing her by calling her “Matilda,” although Penelope becomes visibly uncomfortable with the joke.

When Gabriel asks about the Nameless, Ede admits that even though he and Ade are highly skilled white-hat hackers, they know very little about them. Ade explains that they are not looking too hard because the Nameless are extremely dangerous.

The twins explain that the Nameless kidnap, hurt and kill people for money rather than using their skills to put things right. Ede jokes about how much money they could make, but Ade immediately hits him because of their family rule against talking rubbish.

They explain that the Nameless:

* Have no crew name.
* Never use codenames.
* Never repeat the same con.
* Are therefore extremely difficult to track.

=== Ede’s Humour and Personality ===
Ede is playful, energetic and constantly joking. During Tricks of the Trade, he compares Gabriel’s situation to Oliver Twist.

He and Ade also struggle during the walk through the woods. Ade jokes that he is having a heart attack, while Ede jokes about Ade dying and them having to change their tag.

When choosing their garden patches, the twins continue bickering and fooling around. Ede even throws a caterpillar at Ade, causing him to run away.

Ede frequently uses humour to tease his friends. He calls Penelope a “robot” because of her disciplined sleeping habits and energetic personality.

When Penelope says that food is only fuel, Ede strongly disagrees. He believes food is one of the best things in life and claims that good food is better than breaking through an apparently impossible firewall.

He then jokes that Penelope might actually be a robot and tries to poke her shoulder. Before he can touch her, Penelope grabs his wrist and twists it into an uncomfortable position. Ede quickly learns that touching Penelope without permission is a bad idea, while Ade finds the situation hilarious.

Ede is amazed when Friedrich demonstrates parkour and jokes that his own body could never bend in the same ways.

He also jokes that their mother has been preparing them for Friedrich’s wall sits their entire lives. He claims that he once stayed in a wall sit for so long that he fell asleep.

During Forgery, Ede secretly uses his paintbrush to annoy Ade while Palombo is not looking. He also jokes that a skilled forger is a “crook of all trades.” Palombo agrees and uses the joke to explain how forgery makes a crook adaptable.

Ede is particularly excited about attending Whisper’s class. He and Ade become so enthusiastic that their attempted high five ends with them getting tangled together.

=== Technology and Hacking Skills ===
Ede and Ade are exceptionally talented with technology. In Whisper’s class, they can disassemble and reassemble devices almost effortlessly and keep pace with Whisper while the rest of the class struggles.

Their ability to work together is one of the clearest signs of their extraordinary technical skill.

Ede keeps wearing his skull-and-crossbones earrings and jokes that their hacking is “online piracy.” Ade immediately points out that this is not actually what they do, leading to another typical Okoro argument.

The twins later discover a way around Crookhaven’s communication restrictions. Crookhaven scrambles signals, making phones and most modern communication devices useless, but the twins discover that radios are an overlooked exception.

They find a way to greatly increase the radio's range and smuggle the equipment past the Gardeners by hiding it inside Sneaks.

When Gabriel asks whether the radio is inside their robot, Ade corrects him: Sneaks itself is the radio.

=== The Jollof-Rice Business ===
Ede is enthusiastic about the twins’ jollof-rice business. He believes there is enough money in it for them to become businessmen rather than crooks.

He is particularly excited about expanding their mother’s supply and finding better ways to deliver the food to Crookhaven.

When Decome throws away Gabriel’s food, Ede is angry. He helps prepare Gabriel a bowl of chicken jollof rice from their hidden supply while joking with Ade about their prices and pretending they are serious businessmen.

=== Crimnastics and the Assault Course ===
Ede claims that people back home call him “Black Thunder.” Ade immediately challenges the claim, and the twins begin arguing.

Ede then insists that Gabriel should call him “Black Lightning.”

During the assault course, Ede and Ade decide to go together but disastrously collide on the monkey bars. Their final time is 7.28, making them by far the slowest students in the class.

=== Rumours About Penelope’s Mother ===
Ede is the first to tell Gabriel about the rumour concerning Penelope’s mother.

He explains that he and Ade overheard Upper Delinquents discussing it while testing a rebuilt bug for Whisper. According to the rumour, Penelope’s mother was taken by the Nameless.

Ede wonders why Penelope has never told the twins about it, especially since they could potentially help her investigate.

The twins later reveal that they already knew about Penelope’s mother because their listening devices had picked up conversations around Crookhaven. They promise to help investigate the Nameless, although they worry that the Nameless may now be angry with them because they accidentally recorded one of their conversations.

Ede is proud when Gabriel says he has improved significantly in Infiltration. Ade immediately reminds him that “much better” still means Gabriel used to be terrible.

The twins then get into another chaotic fight before Penelope separates them.

Ede also works with Ade to get Sneaks functioning as a way to contact home. Crookhaven has almost no usable signal, and the terrible weather makes their attempts even harder.

The twins confirm that Sneaks has an EMP capable of producing a two-metre pulse. Ede becomes excited when Gabriel starts thinking about using it during the Break-in.

=== Joining Gabriel’s Team ===
Ede enthusiastically agrees to join Gabriel and Amira.

He jokes that the twins are like musical and rap geniuses when it comes to technology but are hopeless at almost everything else. This highlights how the different members of the team bring different abilities.

Like Ade, Ede doubts that Penelope will agree to join because she wants to complete the Break-in alone. He also expects her to try to take control if she does join.

Ede and Ade suffer badly from hay fever while walking through the woods and can barely see.

They agree that Gabriel should stop waiting for Penelope and ask Ji-a instead. Ede points out that Penelope has rejected Gabriel in numerous languages and believes she has made her decision.

He and Ade also joke about Lulu and Dorian's strange habit of walking beside each other without actually talking.

Ede and Ade continue handling the technological side of the investigation and work on Sneaks.

They enhance a mysterious recording they intercepted, making the male voice clearer. Both twins think the voice sounds like Mr Velasquez, although they agree that the voices are not identical enough to be certain.

Ede is the one who shares the importance of the recorded voices after Ade plays the recording. Like his brother, he immediately recognises the possible connection.

Their usual bickering continues, including an argument over whether the expression is “chalk and cheese” or “chalk and chips.”

Despite constantly arguing, they remain highly capable as a team and are confident that Sneaks is ready for the Break-in.

=== The Break-in ===
Ede and Ade handle the technology during the Break-in.

They use Sneaks to disable Orwell, the crow-camera, with an electromagnetic pulse. This temporarily knocks Orwell out of action and gives Amira enough time to complete the theft.

The twins also record the entire operation and project the footage for the Crooklings to see.

Whisper immediately recognises how clever their timing was, noting that the EMP lasted just long enough to avoid making her suspicious.

Ede and Ade each earn 50 points and gain the right to ask Caspian a truthful question.

Their success confirms their reputation as exceptional hackers and inventors.

Ede immediately helps Gabriel when Adria triggers the library's emergency system.

He and Ade use Sneaks to hold the door open so Gabriel can escape through the narrowing gap.

Their intervention comes at a cost: Sneaks is caught in the crushing door and badly damaged. The twins cry out in pain when their robot is damaged, but they put themselves and their equipment at risk to save Gabriel.

Ede and Ade insist on holding a funeral for Sneaks because they believe the robot sacrificed itself to save Gabriel.

Their dramatic grief leads to another argument with Penelope, who is mainly concerned with getting to lunch.

=== End of the Term ===
Ede and Ade leave for the holidays still arguing about Sneaks and joking about rebuilding her.

They remain determined to investigate the Nameless and continue using their technological skills to uncover the truth.

They also promise to return the following term with even more jollof.

Overall, Ede is portrayed as a highly intelligent, inventive and skilled hacker whose playful, energetic personality contrasts with his serious abilities. Although he and Ade constantly argue, their teamwork is one of their greatest strengths. Ede is loyal to his friends, willing to take risks to help them, and motivated by his desire to expose wrongdoing.

== History ==

=== Gabriel’s Early Life and His Parents ===
Gabriel Avery grows up knowing very little about his parents. They abandoned him when he was very young, leaving him in the care of his grandmother. Gabriel does not even call them Mum and Dad. To him they are simply “Mother” and “Father,” because he feels that people who left him behind did not earn those names. For most of his childhood, he has accepted that they are gone and that he will probably never know who they really were.

His grandmother, whom Gabriel simply calls Grandma, becomes the person who raises him. She provides the stability his parents never gave him, even though their life together is difficult. Gabriel and Grandma move between villages repeatedly, never staying anywhere for very long. Eventually, about a year before the beginning of the main story, they settle in the small village of Torbridge.

Life in Torbridge is poor. Gabriel and Grandma have to work hard to get by, and Gabriel becomes increasingly useful to her. He helps with gardening and cleaning, including work at the Mercier mansion. Survival matters. His difficult childhood also contributes to his extraordinary memory. Gabriel remembers things with unusual accuracy, although he later explains that this is not always a blessing because it means he cannot easily forget painful experiences.

Gabriel's childhood circumstances also lead him into crime. He becomes a talented pickpocket, not because he is naturally cruel or greedy, but because stealing can provide money when he and Grandma need it. Over time, he becomes skilled at approaching people unnoticed, distracting them, reading their movements, and removing objects without being detected. He is already unusually good at the work by the time the story begins.

=== The Mystery of His Parents ===
Although Gabriel rarely speaks about his parents, Grandma knows more about their disappearance than he does. During one of their conversations, she reveals that the names Gabriel has always known may not have been their real names. The police searched for them after they disappeared but could find no trace of them. To Grandma, they were almost like ghosts: people who had existed but seemed to leave no reliable evidence behind.

This information stays with Gabriel. At first, he does not know what to do with it. He has spent years believing that his parents simply abandoned him, but the possibility that even their names were false makes their disappearance seem stranger. His parents were not merely absent; they had apparently been able to disappear completely.

Gabriel later discovers that his parents were not ordinary people who simply abandoned him: they were members of the mysterious and dangerous criminal organisation known as the Nameless. His father, whom Gabriel had known as Federico Guerrero, was actually named Luciano Lopes, a former Crookhaven student and exceptionally talented criminal. Gabriel also discovers that his mother is Adria Vivas. Caspian reveals that Gabriel's parents had stayed at the same hotel where Gabriel was abandoned before disappearing, and that their identities had been difficult to trace because they used false names. 

== The School for Thieves ==

=== The Pickpocketing Test ===
Gabriel's life changes when he encounters a silver-haired stranger in Torbridge. Before meeting him, Gabriel has already attracted the attention of Mrs Evans, a suspicious local woman who seems to have noticed that something is not quite right about him. Gabriel remains alert, but then spots the silver-haired man carrying a wallet.

Gabriel deliberately decides to pickpocket him. He pretends to drop his unusual burnt-looking two-pence coin and bumps into the man. When the stranger bends down to retrieve the coin, Gabriel secretly takes his wallet. It seems like a successful theft.

When Gabriel opens the wallet, however, he finds almost nothing. There is only £10 and a mysterious card with the message, “YOU’RE GOOD. I’M BETTER.”

Gabriel immediately realizes that something is wrong. He rushes back towards the station to find the man, but the stranger has already boarded the departing train. From the train, the silver-haired man holds up what appears to be Gabriel’s unusual two-pence coin.

Gabriel checks his own pocket and discovers that the coin he has is not his. The stranger has somehow stolen Gabriel’s special coin while allowing Gabriel to believe that he was the one doing the stealing. Gabriel has been tested without realizing it.

=== The Mercier Mansion ===
Gabriel continues working with Grandma at the Mercier mansion. While they clean the property, Gabriel is confronted by Charles about his father's missing fedora. Gabriel knows more about the missing hat than he admits: he has secretly stolen it himself.

The incident shows Gabriel's continuing tendency to steal even when he does not necessarily need to. He is curious, impulsive, and confident in his abilities. Eventually, he puts on the fedora and looks at himself in the mirror. The moment becomes symbolic. Gabriel is beginning to imagine himself as someone capable of entering a larger and more dangerous world.

=== Leaving Torbridge ===
Gabriel eventually decides to follow the mystery surrounding the silver-haired man. He sneaks onto a train without buying a ticket. When he realizes he may be caught, he uses his pickpocketing skills to steal Mrs Davenport’s unstamped ticket and uses it to avoid being challenged.

He gets off at the mysterious Moorheart Station. At first, he believes he has simply been tricked. The station seems strange and deserted, and there is no obvious explanation for why the silver-haired man wanted him there.

Gabriel soon finds a strange metal token hidden inside a bronze statue. The statue appears to point towards a concealed gate. Gabriel uses the token to open it and follows a mysterious path into the woodland beyond.

The journey leads him to a huge lake. In the middle of it stands an enormous building on an island. Gabriel follows the path around the water and eventually realizes that the silver-haired man is waiting for him there.

=== Penelope and Caspian Crook ===
Before reaching the island, Gabriel encounters a confident, strange girl named Penelope Crook. She fascinates and intimidates him because she behaves as though she belongs in this mysterious world. When Mickey asks Gabriel for clearance, Gabriel tries to bluff his way through, but Penelope saves him by producing his security card.

Gabriel travels with her to the island and finally meets the silver-haired man properly. His name is Caspian Crook, one of Crookhaven’s leaders and Penelope’s father.

Gabriel is confused by the school's strange vocabulary, including words such as “Merit” and “Legacy.” Caspian explains that Crookhaven is a secret school for talented young criminals. Students are taught unusual skills connected to crime, including forgery, deception, art, physical training, climbing, parkour, infiltration, lock work, and pickpocketing.

For Gabriel, the discovery is extraordinary. At ordinary schools, his abilities have made him different. At Crookhaven, they are the reason he was invited.

Caspian also explains Crookhaven’s philosophy. The school does not believe that every criminal act is morally equal. Some wealthy or powerful people commit serious wrongdoing and escape punishment. Crookhaven therefore believes that sometimes it is necessary to do something wrong in order to put something else right.

Gabriel learns that the school has watched him for years. Former students, called alumni, search for talented young people called Merits. Caspian personally tested Gabriel by deliberately making himself an easy target. Gabriel passed when he successfully stole Caspian’s wallet.

Gabriel initially decides that he should leave. His first loyalty is to Grandma, and he does not want to abandon her. Caspian assures him that he will be able to communicate with her weekly and visit during holidays, although he must keep Crookhaven’s location and purpose secret.

=== A New Life at Crookhaven ===
Gabriel now faces the problem of explaining his disappearance to Grandma. He cannot tell her that he has joined a school for criminals because Caspian has forbidden him from revealing Crookhaven’s secrets. Instead, Gabriel writes that he has received a scholarship to a boarding school.

He explains that he left without saying goodbye because he knew Grandma would try to persuade him to stay. He also arranges for Mr Hartley and his nephew to help her with the cleaning. He promises to return at Christmas and sends his old jumper with the letter so she will know it truly came from him.

As Gabriel explores Crookhaven, he discovers that almost everything has a hidden purpose. Even the toilets are designed to provide lock-picking practice. The Gardeners, Crookhaven alumni who maintain the island, secretly move objects to test students’ memories.

He meets identical twins Ade and Ede Okoro, known as the Brothers Crim. They are famous white-hat hackers who expose corruption and improve security. They have also built Sneaks, a small machine capable of travelling through water, across land, and through the air.

Gabriel becomes friends with the twins and with Penelope. He learns that Merits have earned their places at Crookhaven, while Legacies are children of famous criminals. The students compete for the Crooked Cup throughout the year. The biggest challenge is the Break-in, which offers fifty points and the right to ask Caspian one question, provided the answer will not endanger Crookhaven or its alumni.

=== The First Lessons ===
During the welcoming ceremony, Gabriel meets the other new students and watches the Legacies cross a newly raised path. Each Crookling receives a glowing pebble to throw into the lake. Gabriel is given one by a small girl wearing a red and gold hijab. He also notices another mysterious girl arriving at the end of the ceremony.

Caspian explains why the Merits were selected. Gabriel is especially affected when Caspian says that some students stole because they were trying to survive or provide a better life for someone they loved. Gabriel realizes that Caspian understands his own relationship with Grandma. Instead of feeling ashamed of his past, he feels proud.

His first lessons show him how different Crookhaven is from an ordinary school. In Deception, Mr Khan teaches students about disguises, accents, and becoming another person so completely that even family members might not recognize them. Gabriel initially remains quiet rather than guessing where Mr Khan is from, and is rewarded when the teacher reveals that his accent was fake.

At lunch, Gabriel thinks mainly about having enough food. Remembering Grandma’s saying, “Eat what you can, can what you can’t,” he tries to save leftovers in a can. He later asks Ade and Ede about the Nameless. They explain that the Nameless are exceptionally skilled criminals who use their talents to hurt people for money. Gabriel becomes fascinated because they were once Tier One.

=== Discovering His Strengths ===
In Tricks of the Trade, Gabriel recognizes that the objects covering Mr Velasquez’s classroom walls are items the teacher successfully stole from people. When the Crooked Cup is explained, Gabriel becomes determined to help a Merit win it for the first time in eleven years.

He demonstrates his skill when Decome attempts to pickpocket Mr Velasquez and makes several mistakes. Gabriel explains that Decome approaches from the front, moves too quickly, and ignores his surroundings. Velasquez asks Gabriel to demonstrate.

Gabriel creates a fake story about being lost near Buckingham Palace and uses a map as misdirection. While keeping Velasquez occupied, he secretly takes three tokens. Two are worth one point each and one is worth three. However, he discovers that the third token actually belongs to Decome and was taken when Decome accidentally nudged him.

Gabriel has technically broken the rules before the Crooked Cup has begun. Velasquez gives him a chance to repair the mistake: he must secretly return the token before the next morning.

With Penelope’s help, Gabriel succeeds. They use a fake spider to distract Decome, allowing Gabriel to replace the token in his blazer. Velasquez warns Gabriel that he must learn the difference between being bold and being foolish.

=== Friends, Favourites and Family ===
Gabriel begins choosing the subjects he wants to focus on. He settles on Infiltration, Deception, and Tricks of the Trade. He likes gardening and learns that the Gardeners include famous alumni, but he does not believe Cultivating a Crook will be one of his most important skills. He also decides not to specialize in Crimnastics because Amira is so much better at physical ability.

He becomes increasingly frustrated by the different treatment of Merits and Legacies. In response, he begins secretly pickpocketing Legacy students and earning Crooked Cup points. He avoids stealing from his own classmates, except Penelope, with whom stealing has become a friendly competition.

Gabriel also becomes close to Leon, another Merit who understands hunger and hardship. Leon explains how crews combine different talents, helping Gabriel realize that being exceptional may require working with people whose abilities complement his own. Their shared howl from the roof becomes a symbol of belonging.

Gabriel's friendship with Amira deepens after Villette pulls off her hijab during Crimnastics. Gabriel immediately retrieves it and helps protect Amira. Later, he learns that Ishaan has joined the Nameless, giving him another reason to investigate the group.

Gabriel's regular call with Grandma reassures him that she is safe. Mr Hartley and his son are helping her, the leak in her old room has been repaired, and she is being cared for. When Gabriel returns home, he discovers that Grandma has been forced to leave the Mercier property, but he is relieved when he learns that Mr Hartley has taken her in. Gabriel uses his new skills to recover his grandmother’s mother's ring from the mansion.

=== The Search for His Parents ===
Gabriel cannot stop thinking about his parents. Grandma’s revelation that their names may have been false becomes increasingly important. He searches the alumni records for the names Federico Guerrero and Isabella Cortes, the identities he believes may belong to his parents.

The work becomes exhausting. Gabriel spends hours searching records and sometimes falls asleep over the files. His eidetic memory means the names remain constantly in his mind. He trusts Penelope enough to ask for help, although he keeps his reasons private and promises her a future favour.

Meanwhile, he continues studying the Break-in. He watches other students fail, learning about pressure plates, the crow-camera, security sensors, and weaknesses in the office. He realizes that Sneaks might be able to use an EMP to disable electronic security.

Gabriel eventually finds Federico Guerrero in the alumni records. The file lists another name: Luciano Lopes. Gabriel immediately wonders whether this could be his father’s real identity.

=== Forming the Crew ===
Gabriel decides that the Break-in cannot be completed alone. He forms a team with Amira, Ade, and Ede, and tries to convince Penelope to join them. Penelope refuses and becomes distant from him.

Gabriel is hurt, especially because he thinks she is embarrassed about crying in front of him. He continues studying the office and eventually catches Penelope attempting the Break-in alone. When a crow-camera is about to detect her, Gabriel saves her by distracting it with a pebble.

The experience strengthens Gabriel’s belief that the team needs to work together. His exceptional memory makes him the natural leader. He can mentally reconstruct the entire office and remember every failed attempt by other Crooklings.

Gabriel finally tells his friends about his eidetic memory. He explains that he remembers things “too well” and that the ability can be painful because it preserves memories he would rather forget. His friends understand rather than judging him.

=== The Break-in ===
Gabriel's team eventually carries out the Break-in. His memory and careful observations allow him to coordinate the operation while the others use their own specialized talents. Gabriel understands that Caspian’s confidence in his perfect memory is actually a weakness: if Caspian believes nobody could steal from him without being noticed, the team can use that certainty against him.

The plan succeeds.

Gabriel publicly reveals that they have stolen something from Caspian’s office. Although he is terrified, his confidence holds. The success earns the team fifty points and gives Gabriel the right to ask Caspian one truthful question.

=== The Truth About the Nameless ===
Caspian confirms that Luciano Lopes was Gabriel’s father. He explains that Crookhaven began investigating Gabriel after an alumni contact reported his unusual coin. Caspian and Whisper discovered that Gabriel had been abandoned at the same hotel where Federico Guerrero and Isabella Cortes had stayed before disappearing.

The revelation about Gabriel’s burnt-looking coin is even more important: it is a Mark, the only known symbol of the Nameless.

The revelation devastates Gabriel. He has spent months trying to understand the mysterious criminal group, only to discover that his own parents are connected to it. Worse, Caspian admits that Gabriel was partly recruited as bait. Penelope’s mother had been kidnapped by the Nameless three years earlier, apparently to prove that they could outsmart Caspian. Caspian hoped that spreading news of Gabriel’s talent would eventually draw his parents back.

Gabriel suspects that Mr Velasquez is Luciano because of the mysterious recording, but Velasquez proves that the voice is not his. He nevertheless knew Luciano at Crookhaven and tells Gabriel that his father was exceptionally talented. He also reveals Luciano’s partnership with Adria Vivas.

=== Gabriel Finds His Mother ===
Gabriel’s biggest shock comes when he discovers that Marie-Elise, the librarian who has helped him search the alumni records, is actually his mother, Adria Vivas.

Adria has disguised herself completely. Her eyes are now green rather than amber, her voice is deeper, and her friendly librarian personality disappears. Gabriel notices that her gloves have concealed her fingerprints and skin.

Adria explains that she originally came to Crookhaven to recruit young criminals for the Nameless. Gabriel was a “happy accident.” Once she discovered that he was Luciano’s son, however, he became important to her. She argues that Gabriel naturally resembles his parents and belongs with the Nameless.

Gabriel desperately wants answers. He wants to know why his parents abandoned him, what their real lives were, and what his own true identity might be. Yet when Adria offers him the chance to leave Crookhaven, Gabriel refuses.

Adria attacks his escape route, setting her file on fire and triggering the library’s powerful suction system. Gabriel’s friends risk themselves to help him escape. He follows Adria to the lake and sees her leave with a masked man whose eyes match his own.

=== Choosing His Real Family ===
Gabriel is devastated by the truth, but he eventually decides that Adria and Luciano are not his family simply because they are his biological parents. Grandma is the person who raised him, protected him, fed him, and stayed with him through every difficult part of his childhood.

He tells his friends that his parents are members of the Nameless. He also supports Penelope when she reveals the truth about her own mother. The five friends agree to investigate the Nameless together, even if doing so means breaking Crookhaven’s rules and entering forbidden places.

Gabriel ultimately loses the Crooked Cup to Penelope by only two points. He is not especially disappointed. Winning the Break-in and using his question to uncover the truth about his parents matters far more.

=== The Final Warning ===
Gabriel returns to Torbridge and feels genuinely at home with Grandma. For the first time, he has a clearer understanding of who he is. He is not defined by his parents, nor by the fact that he is a thief. He has found friends, a community, and a place where his abilities can be used for something greater.

Then he receives a postcard showing a wolf pack. Its message echoes Leon’s lesson that becoming exceptional alone is different from becoming unstoppable together.

Gabriel immediately suspects the postcard came from Leon.

That creates one final, terrifying possibility: Leon may have chosen to join the Nameless rather than being kidnapped.

Even worse, the postcard proves that the Nameless know where Gabriel lives.

His new life at Crookhaven has given him belonging, friendship, purpose, and answers about his past. But it has also connected him directly to the most dangerous criminals in his world, leaving Gabriel and his friends with a new mystery and a much greater threat ahead.

== History ==

=== Family, Childhood, and Ambition ===
Penelope is Caspian Crook’s daughter and grew up at Crookhaven. Her unusual upbringing meant that the school and its world were familiar to her from childhood. Leon remembers her sneaking into older students’ classes when she was a Crookling and sitting at the back, apparently enjoying being around people her own age. He was also present when the events surrounding her mother occurred, although Penelope did not explain what had happened to him until much later. Leon considers her the closest thing to a little sister he will ever have.

Penelope’s mother was kidnapped by the Nameless three years before the beginning of the school year. Her disappearance remains one of the deepest sources of pain in Penelope’s life. Caspian never says her name, and Penelope hates Christmas because it reminds her of her mother, who loved the holiday. Penelope keeps wearing a shabby violet ribbon her mother gave her. She believes that if her mother ever sees her again, the ribbon will allow her to recognise Penelope even if she does not recognise her daughter’s face.

The disappearance also shaped Penelope’s ambitions. Her mother is the only person who has ever successfully completed the Robins’ Break-in by entering Crookhaven itself, and even Caspian does not know how she achieved it. Penelope intends to surpass her mother and eventually accomplish the same feat. Although she wants to become the greatest crook ever to live, she refuses to leave Crookhaven before graduating, unlike Marianna Sonorov, who left over Christmas and never returned.

Penelope was raised with exceptional discipline and developed a huge range of abilities. She speaks five languages fluently and understands two more. She trains regularly, later studying Korean and practising free diving, skating, running, and other physical skills. She is also exceptionally knowledgeable about Crookhaven, its history, its traditions, its alumni, and the skills expected of a crook. Her ambition is not simply to be talented; she wants to become the best.

Despite being Caspian’s daughter, Penelope is not simply a copy of him. Leon believes she is different from her father, particularly because she does not look down on Merits. In fact, the people she becomes closest to are Merits. Her greatest hunger is not physical but personal: she desperately wants to prove herself and become the greatest crook in history.

== The School for Thieves ==

=== Arrival and the Merits ===
Penelope is first seen by the lake practising Cantonese. She immediately appears confident, intelligent, disciplined, and unusually capable. Although she initially teases Gabriel, she soon helps him by secretly taking his security card and showing Mickey, the temporary boatman, that Gabriel is authorised to enter Crookhaven. Mickey’s unusually strong reaction to Penelope’s clearance card makes sense when it is revealed that she is Caspian’s daughter.

Penelope’s confidence is both one of her greatest strengths and one of her weaknesses. When she arrives late for a painting lesson, Caspian makes her carry the painting equipment back to the villa herself. She struggles repeatedly but eventually works out a more efficient way to carry everything. Caspian uses the experience to teach her that talent is not enough when it turns into overconfidence. Penelope is annoyed, but she learns from the lesson.

A few days later, Penelope meets the Brothers Crim while they are talking to Gabriel. She praises their Con-Oil job as a masterpiece and explains that she knows how they exposed Hydro-Cell for dumping toxic waste off a coast. She also knows why Ade and Ede became hackers: their father was badly injured because his company used faulty equipment, and the twins wanted to expose the company. Penelope tries not to reveal that Caspian is her father, but Gabriel accidentally lets the secret slip. Ade and Ede become suspicious, while Penelope explains the Crooked Cup and talks about Whisper.

Penelope watches the first-year Legacies arrive even though she has been placed with the Merits. She explains that she has seen the special path across the lake raised only around twenty times and had hoped this would finally be her opportunity to cross it as a Legacy. Caspian decides that she should remain with the Merits because she has lived at Crookhaven all her life. Penelope is clearly disappointed but tries to hide it.

Whisper secretly gives her a small mother-of-pearl USB drive disguised as an earring. Penelope is delighted by the gift. She then listens to Caspian’s speech with the other students and participates in the ceremony by throwing her pebble into the lake. Although she remains disappointed about missing the official Legacy welcome, she is excited about beginning another year. She is placed in Class 1B with Gabriel, Ade, and Ede, making the beginning of the year more enjoyable.

=== Lessons, Rivalry, and Friendship ===
Penelope is immediately excited by the Break-in. She remembers Caspian’s principle that anyone can steal, but only a highly skilled crook can steal without the target noticing anything is missing. When the co-Headmasters’ office is announced as the target, she immediately studies the building, examining its windows, roof, and front door for possible ways inside. Gabriel thinks she might have tried to break in immediately if she had been alone.

In Deception, Penelope already knows that Mr Khan is from Harrow in London because of her familiarity with Crookhaven. She deliberately keeps quiet so that she does not spoil the lesson. When the other students make wrong guesses, she becomes visibly frustrated. Mr Khan eventually reveals the answer and praises her for not giving it away. Later, when asked what Edgar Decome has learned, Penelope says that overconfidence is the enemy of the crook, one of Headmaster Crook’s favourite lessons. Mr Khan challenges this idea, explaining that some overconfidence is necessary when pretending to be another person.

Penelope also excels in History. She confidently gives the exact date Crookhaven was founded, September 30th, 1829, and remembers that the Metropolitan Police Service was founded only one day later. She knows the school’s principles and listens closely to the explanation of the Tiers. When Tier One crooks appear in a projection, she becomes intensely focused on one woman. Gabriel quietly says, “Much is expected of a Crook,” and the woman is revealed to be Penelope’s mother.

At lunch, Gabriel mentions the Nameless. Penelope immediately warns that they are secretive and dangerous and says people at Crookhaven simply do not discuss them. She becomes increasingly frustrated as Gabriel and the twins continue. She explains that many people in the Underworld will not even acknowledge the Nameless’ existence. When Gabriel asks whether she believes they are real, Penelope says she knows they exist and then tells them they should stop discussing the subject.

In Tricks of the Trade, Penelope listens to the explanation of the Crooked Cup. She proudly points out that Legacies have won for eleven consecutive years, then becomes embarrassed by her own boast. During Gardening, she is frustrated because she expects to study useful plants and substances rather than simply garden. She mentions herbs that can cause unconsciousness and fungi that can cause temporary paralysis, but Mr Sisman explains that Crooklings do not study such things yet. The lesson is really about patience and discipline. Penelope even corrects his attribution of a quotation about patience.

She explains that Gardeners are Crookhaven alumni and that serving as one for a year is an honour. Penelope has no desire to become one because she intends to become the greatest crook ever. She chooses a garden patch beside Gabriel’s and notices the token in his pocket. By seeing the initials, she realises he has Decome’s token and understands that he must return it before morning.

Rather than expose him, Penelope helps him. She wants to beat Gabriel fairly, without him blaming a penalty if she wins the Crooked Cup. She creates a distraction at dinner by loudly claiming that there is a spider on Decome’s back while Gabriel pretends to remove it. The plan succeeds, and Decome never notices that his token has been returned.

Penelope is highly competitive but also fair. She eats quickly because she regards food as fuel and reacts instantly when Ede tries to poke her, grabbing his wrist and stopping him. She continues competing with Gabriel, picking his pockets every day while returning his tokens, just as he returns hers. Her rivalry gradually becomes friendship. Caspian tells Gabriel that Penelope likes him and the twins, and Gabriel realises that he has finally made genuine friends after years of moving from town to town.

=== Physical Skill and Personal Discipline ===
Penelope’s discipline appears in her daily routine. She wears jogging gear, ties her long brown hair into a ponytail with her violet ribbon, and jokes that she gets eight hours of sleep every night. Ade and Ede call her a robot. She accompanies Gabriel to Crimnastics, claiming that Friedrich asked her to make sure Class 1B could find the building. Gabriel suggests she is simply looking after her “partner in crime,” but Penelope insists that she does not need a partner or anyone else.

Her ability is demonstrated repeatedly. During a climbing competition, Villette chooses her for a team, and Penelope completes the climb in thirty-two seconds, beating Gabriel by one second. In Crimnastics she completes an assault course in 2.24, determined to stay ahead. When Gabriel jokes that he could have tripped deliberately so she could win, she becomes furious because she wants a genuine victory. She reminds him that she helped return Decome’s token specifically so the competition would remain fair.

Penelope is similarly disciplined in Forgery. While the other students are disorganised, she sits opposite Gabriel and gives them an icy look when Palombo stops the lesson. She recognises which paintings are original and which are forged but deliberately holds back. Caspian later teases her, saying that if she worked as hard at brushwork as she did at glaring, she would be top of the class. He also points out that Oh Ji-a, a Merit, is particularly talented at Forgery.

Leon’s memories further reveal Penelope’s personality. He says that she has known him since childhood and that he watched her deal with the aftermath of her mother’s disappearance. He also recognises that her ambition is unusually intense. Unlike some Legacies, she does not consider herself superior to Merits, and her friendship with Gabriel, Ade, and Ede proves it.

Penelope also helps Gabriel search the Crookhaven alumni records, even though she knows he has a personal reason for wanting them. She leads him to a secluded room containing records of every heist, gambit, and former student connected to the school. She warns him about the security system and makes clear that the records cannot simply be removed or destroyed.

=== Conflict, the Break-in, and Trust ===
Penelope is among the first students to react angrily when Villette attacks Amira. She helps Gabriel shield Amira and later tells Villette that her actions were cruel. Her loyalty to her friends becomes increasingly important.

When Gabriel returns to Crookhaven, Penelope meets him in the woods and immediately becomes interested when he says he has learned his parents’ names. She continues her strict routine of HIIT and language study and has recently begun learning Korean, already managing short conversations with Ji-a.

Penelope’s confidence also causes conflict. During the Crooked Cup, she leads Gabriel by ten points. When Gabriel suggests Caspian might be helping Villette because he wants a Legacy to win, Penelope becomes furious. She insists that she is not a cheat and promises that if she discovers her father and Villette are cheating, she will stop them.

Penelope reveals that the Robins’ Break-in is to enter Crookhaven itself and that her mother is the only person ever to succeed. She intends to repeat the achievement. She is also determined to win the current Break-in. She visits Gabriel in the library partly to tease him about apparently giving up, sarcastically asking whether he is really going to let her win and calling him her toughest competition.

Her determination becomes more personal when she attempts the Break-in alone. After a week in which she avoids Gabriel because she had cried in front of him, she secretly enters the course. She successfully navigates pressure plates and motion sensors and reaches the office. She notices the danger at the eastern window and starts climbing toward the roof, but Gabriel stops her before she is caught by the crow-camera. Penelope insists at first that she did not need his help. Her attitude changes when he tells her he has found something that may identify his father.

Penelope eventually accepts that the Break-in cannot be completed alone. During Cultivating a Crook, her garden is shown to be extremely organised, with flowers arranged by colour and many plants having thorns or spiky leaves. After watching other Crooklings fail, she joins Gabriel’s team with Amira and the Brothers Crim. She admits that Gabriel was right: entering the Headmasters’ office alone is almost impossible, but a crew combining different abilities might succeed.

Penelope initially wants to enter through the chimney because of her Crimnastics ability. Gabriel insists that Amira is better suited to the physical entry. Penelope is frustrated by Gabriel’s leadership but eventually asks what her own strength will be. He tells her that everything is within her strengths. She rolls her eyes but accepts the role.

She also tells the group more about her mother and Caspian. Caspian remembers every mistake Penelope has made and can use those memories against her, while somehow forgetting things whenever it suits him. Her mother has been gone for three years, and Caspian has never said her name. The violet ribbon remains Penelope’s link to her.

The team’s plan succeeds. Penelope forges Caspian’s Matisse painting so convincingly that he initially believes it is the original. When he discovers that she created the replacement, he calls it “absolutely … exquisite.” His praise overwhelms her, and tears come to her eyes. She earns fifty points and the right to ask Caspian one truthful question. She and Amira celebrate the successful Break-in.

=== The Nameless and Penelope’s Mother ===
The danger surrounding Gabriel’s investigation soon brings the friends together again. Penelope helps protect him when Adria threatens to burn the file, pushing the others back and obeying the warning because she understands the library’s security. When the library begins sucking the air from the room, Penelope and the others hold the door open long enough for Gabriel to escape. At the lake, Gabriel finally identifies the escaping pair as his parents.

Penelope remains involved in the group’s investigation into the Nameless, despite being frustrated by Sneaks’ funeral because she wants lunch. Eventually, she tells Gabriel, Ade, Ede, and Amira the truth she has carried for three years: her mother was kidnapped by the Nameless.

The confession is a major turning point. Her friends already suspected or knew much of the truth, but Penelope had never openly admitted it. Once she says it aloud, she seems relieved. She asks whether they will really help her discover what happened. The group promises to help, even if doing so means breaking rules. Penelope agrees firmly, saying that for her mother she will do whatever is necessary.

=== Growth Beyond Talent ===
By summer, Penelope has not lost her confidence or ambition. Instead, she has begun to understand how to control them. She finishes the Crooked Cup only two points ahead of Gabriel, proves herself during the Break-in, earns meaningful praise from Caspian, and gains friends who are willing to stand beside her. Her decision to keep training and studying shows that she sees success as something that must be continually earned. Her story therefore ends not with her becoming the greatest crook, but with her determined to keep learning until she can become one.

Als Baby wurde er von seinen Eltern unter mysteriösen Umständen verlassen, daraufhin hat ihn die liebenswerte ältere Frau, ohne zu zögern, bei sich aufgenommen. Er wuchs fortan bei seiner „Großmutter“ auf. Doch nun leben die beiden in sehr ärmlichen Verhältnissen. Nicht selten überlässt die Oma ihrem Enkel auch ihren Anteil an einer Mahlzeit. Gabriel nutzt daher seine erstklassigen diebischen Fähigkeiten regelmäßig, um sich und seiner Großmutter ein wenig Geld dazuzuverdienen. Nur bei dem einen Diebstahl am Bahnhof wird er von dem fremden Mann mit silbrigen Haaren überlistet. Aber nicht nur das – statt einer Strafanzeige erhält er von ebendiesem Mann eine rätselhafte Einladung inklusive eines Vollstipendiums für die äußerst geheime Schule „Crookhaven“. Dieses Internat liegt verborgen auf einer Insel und ist darauf ausgerichtet, angehende Gauner mit einzigartigen Diebeskünsten oder mit berühmt-berüchtigten Gauner-Eltern zu Meisterdieben auszubilden. Jedoch dient das dortige Training ausschließlich dem Zweck, etwas Positives zu bewirken.
Ausgerechnet an diesem Ort für Außenseiter trifft Gabriel auf einige Gleichgesinnte mit ähnlich speziellen Begabungen – und doch hat ein jeder und eine jede von ihnen seine ganz eigenen Stärken. Zum ersten Mal in seinem Leben kann er echte Freundschaften schließen. Gemeinsam mit den neuen Freunden schmiedet er den Plan, den diesjährigen Gaunerpokal aus dem Schulleiterbüro zu stehlen. Können Gabriel und seine Mitschüler:innen diesen unmöglich erscheinenden Wettbewerb gewinnen? Und was hat es mit dem Verschwinden seiner leiblichen Eltern auf sich?
Dieser Auftakt der Reihe rund um Gabriel ist einfach wundervoll geschrieben. Die Charaktere sind herrlich vielfältig und tiefgründig. Die liebevolle Ausarbeitung der Figuren hat mir solch ein Vergnügen beim Lesen bereitet, dass ich das Buch gar nicht mehr aus der Hand legen konnte. Hier und da gab es ein paar kleinere Ähnlichkeiten mit Harry Potter, aber genau das hat mich oft zum Schmunzeln gebracht. Letztendlich ist der Schulalltag im Internat „Crookhaven“ jedoch von J. J. Arcanjo so außergewöhnlich konzipiert, dass er für die jungen Leser:innen so einige Überraschungen bereithält. Zudem sind die Erlebnisse der Schüler:innen mit vielen kleinen und großen lehrreichen Botschaften in angenehm kurze Teilgeschichten verpackt.
Überhaupt fällt es erstaunlich leicht, in die fantasievoll gestaltete Geschichte über Freundschaft, Zusammenhalt und gegenseitiges Vertrauen einzutauchen. Die durchweg fesselnde Handlung hat mich in diesem ersten Band wahrlich mitgerissen. Ein wirklich tolles Buch, das nachdenklich macht und gleichzeitig zum Lachen und Miträtseln einlädt! Mit diesem spannenden Cliffhanger freue ich mich jetzt umso mehr auf das nächste Abenteuer von Gabriel und seinen Freunden.
Eine Schule für Meisterdiebe, auf der man professionelles Täuschen, Stehlen, Schlösserknacken lernt, und das als Jugendbuch? Ich war zunächst skeptisch, ob ich das wirklich als geeignet für meinen Sohn ansehen sollte, doch die Geschichte hat mich absolut überzeugt. Die Schule verfolgt einen Robin-Hood-Ansatz, der das Gaunern nur erlaubt um zu überleben oder wenn hierdurch geschehenes oder bestehendes Unrecht wieder aufgehoben wird.
Den 13jährigen Protagonisten Gabriel Avery schloss ich sofort ins Herz. Generell gefiel mir die Figurenzeichnung im Buch sehr gut. Die Charaktere sind vielschichtig angelegt, und einige lassen Geheimnisse erahnen. Ob ehrgeizige Einzelkämpferin, tollpatschige Techniknerds im Doppelpack oder stilles Kletterass - alle haben ihre Ecken und Kanten, bringen Farbe in die Geschichte und wachsen zusammen. Denn: "Allein kann man außergewöhnlich werden, zusammen unaufhaltsam."
Der Schreibstil ist flüssig und angenehm zu lesen, uns bereits nach wenigen Seiten war ich vollends in die Geschichte abgetaucht. Ein bisschen erinnert das Setting an die klassischen Internatsgeschichten, die seit Jahrzehnten erfolgreich sind und die ich schon in meiner eigenen Jugend liebte, jedoch mit einem wirklich aussergewöhnlichen Hintergrund und einer guten Portion Spannung. Als wohltuend empfand ich angesichts der Flut an Fantasy-Geschichten, dass dieses Buch in der realen Welt spielt.
Der Autor entwirft eine komplexe Geschichte mit mehreren losen Enden und vielen Andeutungen, wobei sehr schnell klar ist, dass nicht alle in diesem Buch aufgelöst werden können, und offenbar eine Fortsetzung oder gar eine Reihe geplant ist. Dennoch ist das Buch in sich rund und ein wichtiger Handlungsstrang wird abgeschlossen, so dass das Buch zwar einen Folgeband andeutet, aber nicht "mittendrin" mit einem Cliffhanger endet.
Fazit: J.J. Arcanjo hat mit der Schule der Meisterdiebe eine wirklich fesselnde und ideenreiche Geschichte geschrieben, die eine perfekte Mischung aus Internatsleben und Spannung bietet. Ich freue mich jetzt schon richtig auf die Fortsetzung und bin gespannt, wie es mit Gabriel und seinen Freunden weitergeht.

Hier und da gab es ein paar kleinere Ähnlichkeiten mit Harry Potter, aber genau das hat mich oft zum Schmunzeln gebracht. Letztendlich ist der Schulalltag im Internat „Crookhaven“ jedoch von J. J. Zudem sind die Erlebnisse der Schüler:innen mit vielen kleinen und großen lehrreichen Botschaften in angenehm kurze Teilgeschichten verpackt.

Im vierten Jahr an der Schule der Meisterdiebe bekommen Gabriel und seine Freunde das neue Fach Umgebungskunde und lernen, unbemerkt an einen anderen Ort zu gelangen. Das hilft, denn dieses Mal gewinnt den Gaunerpokal, wer es schafft, in die berüchtigte Festung Tor Malan einzubrechen und eine der kostbarsten Ketten der Welt zu stehlen. Leider sind hinter dieser Kette auch die mächtigsten Verbrecher der Unterwelt her, die Namenlosen. Und um sich an Gabriel zu rächen, schmieden sie einen üblen Plan ... Nur mit Einfallsreichtum und Hilfe von unerwarteter Seite können Gabriel und seine Bande es schaffen.
